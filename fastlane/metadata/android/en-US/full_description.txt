<p><b>Export To Graphene (ETG)</b> moves your personal data from an old Android phone to <b>GrapheneOS</b>. It works fully offline, needs no Google services and no account, and encrypts everything it moves.</p>

<p><b>What it moves</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Contacts (vCard) with photos</li>
<li>SMS and MMS, call history and blocked numbers</li>
<li>Calendar events with reminders and repeats (iCalendar)</li>
<li>Photos, videos, music, ringtones, documents and downloads</li>
<li>Chat backups (Signal, Molly, WhatsApp, Telegram, Element, Session, SimpleX, SinusChat)</li>
<li>KeePass databases, 2FA exports (Aegis, 2FAS…), Google Authenticator QR codes, VPN and SSH keys</li>
<li>Saved Wi-Fi networks (QR codes, WifiConfigStore.xml or Shizuku)</li>
<li>Installed apps as APK files, with free-software alternatives suggested</li>
<li>System settings: wallpaper, sounds, dark theme, animations, Private DNS, navigation mode, keyboard and more (some need Shizuku)</li>
</ul>

<p><b>How it moves it</b></p>
<ul>
<li>One encrypted backup file (.etg) on the phone or an SD card</li>
<li>Directly to a USB drive (USB-OTG)</li>
<li>Phone to phone over Wi-Fi or Wi-Fi Direct: scan a QR code, touch the phones (NFC) or type a short code</li>
<li>Over a USB cable, or downloaded to a computer in a web browser</li>
</ul>

<p><b>Security</b></p>
<ul>
<li>AES-256-GCM encryption with a key derived using PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 (210,000 iterations)</li>
<li>Phone-to-phone transfer uses ECDH P-256 with a new key every session, and the sending phone must approve the receiver</li>
<li>Every file carries a SHA-256 checksum that is checked on restore</li>
<li>No internet permission is used for anything other than the local transfer between your own devices – no trackers, no analytics</li>
</ul>

<p>You can choose exactly what to move, down to single apps, contacts or settings. If something cannot be moved, ETG skips it, keeps going and at the end shows what failed and why, with an option to try again.</p>

<p>A step-by-step checklist guides you through the whole migration, from saving your 2FA codes and chat backups to installing GrapheneOS and restoring everything.</p>
